### about me  
i’m a **full-stack developer** with deep experience in **web3** and modern frontend frameworks. my expertise spans **react.js, vue.js, next.js, node.js, solidity, rust, and cairo**, allowing me to deliver everything from high-performance user interfaces to complex blockchain integrations.  

i’ve built and scaled applications that handle millions in trading volume, streamline processes for thousands of users, and push the limits of web performance.  

notable achievements include:  
- **winner** of the *ethglobal x starknet hackathon* with **raize club**, an on-chain prediction market on starknet, handling $18k+ in predictions.  
- **winner** of the *encode x solana foundation hackathon* with **hushpay**, an anonymous donation platform leveraging zero-knowledge proofs on solana.  
- architected and deployed uniswap v2 liquidity pools for zetachain’s dex, supporting **$3m in tvl** and **$100k+ in daily trading volume**.  
- designed and delivered high-traffic, revenue-generating web platforms serving **10,000+ users** and enabling multi-crore inr transactions.  

currently, i’m exploring **cross-chain defi protocols, scalable dapps, and the intersection of physics and computer science** while pursuing my dual degree at **bits pilani**.
